Appointments & first visits

Booking, hours, what to bring and where to find the office.

Is Angelo Forte, DMD taking new patients?

Yes. Call (561) 747-2134 to book a first visit. The office will take your details and insurance information over the phone.

Where is the office?

654 W. Indiantown Rd., Suite 105, Jupiter, FL 33458, on Indiantown Road a short distance east of Military Trail. Harbourside Place and Jupiter Medical Center are both under two miles away.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, closed for lunch from 1:00 to 2:00 PM. Friday is by appointment only. The office is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card and photo ID, a list of the medications you take, and any recent dental X-rays from a previous dentist if you have them. Arriving a little early gives time for paperwork.

If I can't make my appointment, what should I do?

Call the office as soon as you can if you need to cancel or will be running late, so the time can be offered to someone else.

Insurance & payment

Plans, out-of-network claims, payment options and financing.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

The practice accepts most traditional insurance plans and will file your claims for you. It does not participate in HMO plans. Call the office with your plan details and they will check it for you.

Can I see you if you're out of network for my plan?

In many cases, yes. If your plan lets you choose your own dentist, the office is happy to file your insurance for reimbursement. Call to check how your plan handles out-of-network care.

How can I pay?

Cash, check and credit cards. The practice also offers a flexible payment plan; ask the Financial Coordinator for details. CareCredit financing is accepted for costs insurance doesn't cover.

Dental emergencies

Toothaches, broken and knocked-out teeth, and when to go to the ER.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call (561) 747-2134. For a severe injury, uncontrolled bleeding, or swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room first. Our emergency page explains what to do with a knocked-out or broken tooth.

What counts as a dental emergency?

A knocked-out or loosened adult tooth, a broken tooth with pain, a severe toothache, swelling in the face or gums, or bleeding that won't stop. Severe facial injuries and swelling that affects breathing or swallowing need the ER first.

More on emergency dental care

What should I do with a knocked-out tooth?

Hold it by the crown, rinse it briefly without scrubbing, and either place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Then call the office immediately; the sooner it is seen, the better the chance of saving it.

More on emergency dental care

Can I go to urgent care for a toothache?

Urgent care and ER doctors can help with pain, swelling and infection, but they can't repair the tooth. Call the dental office so the cause can be treated.

More on emergency dental care

Cleanings, exams & gum health

Check-ups, X-rays, oral cancer screening, sealants and gum disease.

Do you treat children?

The practice cares for the whole family, and its preventive services include sealants to protect children's teeth from decay.

Do you use digital X-rays?

Yes. Images are ready on screen straight away, so Dr. Forte can show you what he sees.

How often do I need a dental cleaning?

Many patients come every six months. If you have a history of gum disease or frequent cavities, Dr. Forte may suggest a shorter interval. He will explain the reason for whatever schedule he recommends.

More on exams & cleanings

Is an oral cancer screening included in my exam?

Oral cancer screening is part of the practice's preventive care. It is a quick visual and hands-on check of the tissues in your mouth and throat.

More on exams & cleanings

Is it normal for gums to bleed when I floss?

Occasional bleeding is common, but regular bleeding is one of the first signs of gum inflammation. Have it checked; early gum disease is usually reversible.

More on gum disease treatment

What is a deep cleaning?

Scaling and root planing cleans below the gumline, removing tartar from the roots and smoothing them so the gums can heal. It is usually done with the area numbed.

More on gum disease treatment

Can gum disease be cured?

Gingivitis can usually be reversed. Periodontitis can be controlled and its progress stopped with treatment and regular maintenance, but bone that has been lost does not simply grow back.

More on gum disease treatment

Fillings, crowns & root canals

Fixing cavities, cracked teeth and infected teeth.

Does getting a filling hurt?

The tooth is numbed before any work begins, so most people feel pressure rather than pain. Some sensitivity for a few days afterwards is common and usually settles on its own. Call the office if it doesn't.

More on fillings

Can old silver fillings be replaced?

Yes. Old fillings that are worn, cracked or leaking can be replaced. Dr. Forte will tell you whether a filling actually needs replacing or is still doing its job.

More on fillings

How long does a filling take?

Most fillings are finished in one appointment. Larger or multiple fillings take longer, and the office will tell you how much time to set aside when you book.

More on fillings

How long does a dental crown last?

A well-made crown on a healthy tooth often lasts many years. How long depends on the material, your bite, grinding habits and how well the area is cleaned. Dr. Forte will check it at every exam.

More on crowns & bridges

Is a bridge or an implant better?

It depends on the teeth next to the gap and the bone where the tooth is missing. A bridge relies on the neighboring teeth; an implant stands on its own. Dr. Forte will explain the trade-offs for your mouth.

More on crowns & bridges

How many visits does a crown take?

Usually two: one to prepare the tooth and fit a temporary, and one to fit the permanent crown.

More on crowns & bridges

Is a root canal painful?

The tooth is fully numbed during treatment. Most of the pain people associate with root canals comes from the infection beforehand, and treatment relieves it. Some tenderness for a few days afterwards is normal.

More on root canal treatment

Do I need a crown after a root canal?

Often, especially on back teeth that take heavy chewing forces. Dr. Forte will tell you if your tooth needs one.

More on root canal treatment

Can an abscessed tooth go away on its own?

No. Antibiotics can calm an infection temporarily, but the source inside the tooth stays until it is treated. Call the office if you have swelling or a painful bump on your gum.

More on root canal treatment

Whitening, veneers & Invisalign

Brightening, reshaping and straightening your smile.

Do you offer Invisalign?

Yes. Invisalign is listed among the practice's equipment, and orthodontic treatment is one of its services.

Does teeth whitening damage enamel?

Whitening carried out as directed under a dentist's supervision is generally considered safe for enamel. Temporary sensitivity is the most common side effect.

More on teeth whitening

Will whitening work on my crowns or veneers?

No. Whitening only changes natural tooth enamel. Crowns, veneers, fillings and bonding keep their original shade.

More on teeth whitening

How long does whitening last?

It varies with diet and habits. Coffee, tea, red wine and tobacco bring stain back sooner. Touch-ups maintain the result.

More on teeth whitening

How long do porcelain veneers last?

Veneers often last many years with good care. Grinding, biting hard objects and gum health all affect how long they last.

More on porcelain veneers

Do veneers look natural?

Porcelain is translucent like enamel, and each veneer is made to match the shape and shade you choose with Dr. Forte, so they are designed to look like your own teeth.

More on porcelain veneers

Should I whiten before veneers?

If you want your other teeth lighter too, whitening first lets the veneers be matched to your brighter shade.

More on porcelain veneers

Is bonding the same as a veneer?

No. Bonding is resin applied and shaped directly on the tooth, usually in one visit. A porcelain veneer is a thin shell made to fit and then bonded on. Veneers resist staining and wear better; bonding is quicker and less involved.

More on dental bonding

Can bonding be whitened?

Resin does not respond to whitening the way enamel does. If you are planning to whiten, it is usually best to whiten first so the bonding can be matched to your new shade.

More on dental bonding

How many hours a day do I wear Invisalign aligners?

Aligners are generally worn for most of the day and night and removed to eat, drink anything other than water, and clean your teeth. Dr. Forte will give you the target for your treatment.

More on Invisalign & Orthodontics

Do I need a retainer afterwards?

Yes. Teeth naturally try to move back, and a retainer keeps them where treatment put them.

More on Invisalign & Orthodontics

Can adults get Invisalign?

Yes. Many people who straighten their teeth with clear aligners are adults.

More on Invisalign & Orthodontics

Implants, dentures & extractions

Replacing missing teeth, and when a tooth has to come out.

How long do dental implants last?

With healthy gums and good cleaning, implants are designed to last for many years. The crown on top may eventually need replacing from normal wear.

More on dental implants

Am I too old for an implant?

Age alone usually isn't the deciding factor. Overall health, bone and gum health matter more. Dr. Forte will assess this at a consultation.

More on dental implants

Does insurance cover implants?

Coverage varies a lot between plans. The office will help you check your benefits and file the claim, and CareCredit financing is available for costs your insurance doesn't cover.

More on dental implants

How long does it take to get used to dentures?

Most people adapt over a few weeks. Sore spots are normal early on and can be adjusted at the office.

More on dentures

Can dentures be made to fit more securely?

Yes. Relining can improve the fit as your mouth changes, and implants can anchor a denture for a much firmer hold. Dr. Forte will tell you whether implants are an option for you.

More on dentures

Does a tooth extraction hurt?

The area is numbed, so you should feel pressure rather than pain during the extraction. Soreness for a few days afterwards is normal and is usually managed with over-the-counter pain relief as directed.

More on tooth extraction

What is a dry socket?

It happens when the blood clot in the socket is lost too early, exposing the bone. It is painful but treatable. Avoiding straws, smoking and hard rinsing in the first days helps prevent it.

More on tooth extraction

TMJ & jaw pain

Clicking, aching jaws and teeth grinding.

Can a dentist treat TMJ pain?

Yes. Dentists commonly diagnose and treat TMD, often with a custom night guard or splint and bite adjustment. Complex joint problems may be referred to a specialist.

More on tmj / tmd treatment

Will a night guard stop me grinding?

A night guard doesn't always stop the habit, but it protects your teeth from wear and can ease the strain on your jaw muscles and joints.

More on tmj / tmd treatment

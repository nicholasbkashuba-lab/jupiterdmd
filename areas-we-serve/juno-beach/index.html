Getting here

From Juno Beach to the office

5.6 miles~10 minutes

Routing estimate from the center of Juno Beach, outside rush hour.

Take US-1 north out of Juno Beach toward Jupiter, then turn west onto Indiantown Road. The office is a little under a mile along, past Alternate A1A.

  • The route is almost entirely US-1, so there is no need to cut inland to Military Trail.
  • Juno Beach Pier is about five miles from the office, roughly ten minutes by car outside rush hour.
